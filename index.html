Points to remember

  • Gains made under investment bonds, whether onshore or offshore, are classed as savings income.
  • Top slicing relief is a tax reducer and is available where a chargeable event gain (CEG) would cause a taxpayer to pay tax at a rate higher than if the CEG wasn't included in their tax calculation for that tax year.
  • In some circumstances, CEGs can be set against any unused:
    • Personal Allowance (PA)
    • Starting Rate for Savings Band (SRSB) of up to £5,000 depending on other income
    • Personal Savings Allowance (PSA) of up to £1,000 depending on income tax band
  • Top slicing relief is not available where trustees or personal representatives are assessed for a chargeable gain.
  • When terminating a bond, the number of complete relevant years is always back to the start of the bond. Where an excess withdrawal has been taken, this can change.
  • For jointly owned policies, use the gain attributable to the person being assessed. Do not enter the same full gain for each owner.
  • Negative gains and capital-loss deductions are not supported. Check possible deficiency relief separately.
