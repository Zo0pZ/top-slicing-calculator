# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Financial advisers supporting UK clients with investment-bond chargeable event gains and Top Slicing Relief calculations.

## Product Purpose

Provide an estimate of tax on onshore and offshore bond gains, with an explainable five-step Top Slicing Relief breakdown advisers can use to review and discuss a calculation.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, and reassuring. Follow the Canada Life UK adviser-support tone.

## Anti-references

Avoid playful or marketing-heavy presentation, opaque single-number results, and unsupported claims of formal tax advice.

## Design Principles

- Make calculations traceable: show inputs, allowances, tax bands, actual credits, notional TSR deductions, and final liability separately.
- Put adviser workflows first: keep repeated calculations direct and make assumptions visible beside the relevant inputs or results.
- Prefer clear, cautious language for tax rules, estimates, and limitations.
- Make the tool usable by keyboard and assistive technology at all viewport sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard operation, visible focus, clear labels and validation, screen-reader status updates, responsive text/layout, and non-color-only meaning.
